# Code of Conduct

OpenParlor is a project about games people play together, often with children in the room. We want everyone who takes
part, in issues, pull requests, discussions and the content we ship, to feel welcome and safe.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intentions, and say so when you disagree.
- Be respectful of different ages, backgrounds, languages, abilities and experience.
- Keep feedback about the work, not the person.
- Keep content suitable for mixed audiences by default. Material meant only for adults must be an optional pack that is
  clearly labelled and never mixed in unless someone chooses it.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language or jokes.
- Sexual or violent content in the default content, or anything that targets a person or group.
- Publishing other people's private information.
- Continuing after someone has asked you to stop.

## Reporting and enforcement

If something happens that breaks this code, tell the maintainers privately: on the repository's **Security** tab choose
**Report a vulnerability** and say it is a conduct report (the form is private to the maintainers). Reports are handled with
discretion.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues, pull requests and content that break this code, and may warn, mute or
ban people who do so repeatedly or seriously. The first response to a report is a private conversation, unless there is a
safety concern.

This code applies in all project spaces and when someone represents the project in public.
